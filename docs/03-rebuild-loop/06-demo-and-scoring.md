# 10. Winning Demo and Storytelling

## Three-act story

### Act 1 — The loss

An office floor is scheduled for demolition in 48 hours. Its doors, lights, ceiling grid, timber and metal are about to be treated as one demolition job. Drawings are incomplete and buyers do not know what will become available.

### Act 2 — The agent intervenes

The site lead uploads a phone walkthrough and one BOQ. ReBuild Loop creates an evidence-linked candidate inventory, but refuses to pretend certainty. It asks for three close-ups and two measurements. The reviewer confirms the inventory. The system separates reusable components from mineral debris, explains pathways and ranks compatible demonstration demand.

### Act 3 — The safe adaptation

New evidence downgrades one door and a proposed buyer rejects another lot. ReBuild Loop revises the plan, blocks unsafe reuse, chooses an alternative channel and updates potential outcomes. A named reviewer approves the final recovery pack.

## Demo rules

- Show one end-to-end project, not every screen.
- Make uncertainty visible.
- Label demonstration demand clearly.
- Do not depend on a live Gemini call for every critical video moment; keep a pre-analysed project available while also supporting a live path.
- Prepare a backup local recording and seeded recovery state.
- Never make the audience read a dense report to understand the win.

## 11. Scoring Strategy

## AI-agent evaluation — 50%

- Demonstrate an actual loop: inspect, ask, revise, act, obtain approval and adapt.
- Show tool boundaries: Gemini extracts; deterministic code decides scores and arithmetic.
- Publish evaluation cases and safety failures caught.
- Show provenance, retry behavior and a human-control boundary.
- Keep the deployed demo reliable and the repository reproducible.

## Community votes — 25%

- Thumbnail: split image of intact materials versus mixed rubble, with a clear “Before demolition” message.
- Agent Card headline: **Save the material before it becomes waste.**
- Use a 30–60 second social clip featuring the changing door decision.
- Show one credible number from the demo as “potential,” with the calculation visible.
- Ask for feedback on a specific product decision, not only for votes.

## Structured feedback — 25%

- Submit by 2 August or earlier.
- Record every feedback item in a small changelog.
- Fix the highest-impact clarity/reliability gaps.
- Update the Agent Card and demo.
- In the final submission, mention concrete changes made from feedback.
